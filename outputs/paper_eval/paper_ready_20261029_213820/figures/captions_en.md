# English figure legends

**Fig. 1 | Official performance across evaluation dimensions.** a, Parsed MCQA option accuracy. b, Strict correct-single-letter accuracy. c, Single-letter format compliance. d, Citation-stripped OpenQA lexical F1. All conditions use the same 227 original questions; the two tasks are not pooled as independent samples. Points show proportions or question-level mean F1, and bars show 95% paired percentile source-group bootstrap intervals (181 groups; 2,000 resamples; seed=20261017). FT denotes the final English v2 epoch_1 adapter. F1 is not factual accuracy, and output format or valid IDs do not establish evidence support. Source data: fig1_formal_source.csv; binary numerators and denominators are in Table 2.

**Fig. 2 | Development-stage trajectories depend on retrieval.** a,b, Citation-stripped F1 without and with BM 25 RAG. c,d, Corresponding mean generated tokens, including EOS. All stages use the same 101 questions, a 160-token ceiling and matched evaluation prompts. F1 intervals are 95% paired source-group bootstrap intervals (91 groups; 10,000 resamples; seed=20261028), pointwise and unadjusted for multiplicity. Lengths are descriptive. Connecting lines indicate stage order, not a causal effect of one training change. This previously selected development set is not an independent test set. Source data: fig2_stages_source.csv.

**Fig. 3 | F1 direction and AI comparative content judgments.** Counts cover 101 paired development v1/v2 RAG 160 answers. Difference is v2−v1, with a 1e −12 threshold; row denominators are 24, 35 and 42, with 101 overall. Columns preserve the original single-AI labels, including trade-off and uncertain. “Worse” may reflect refusal, omission or citation differences rather than a new factual error. No uncertainty interval or scientific ground-truth label is inferred. Source data: fig3_f1_content_source.csv.

**Fig. 4 | Paired changes in erroneous refusal and point coverage.** a, Binary erroneous-refusal transitions across 101 pairs: 93 neither erroneous, 5 newly erroneous, 0 resolved and 3 retained. A1109 among the new cases was only partially answerable and had disputed cost scope. b, Thin lines show individual coverage for 97 assessable questions (overlapping lines may obscure multiplicities); the thick line shows equal-question means, 77.45% to 69.97%. Original pooled-point totals are 104/141 and 93/141. Full/partial coverage receives 1/0.5 credit; 4 NA questions are not zero. These are descriptive existing single-AI labels, without CIs. Necessary-point definitions remain subjective and have not been independently validated by experts. Source data: fig4_paired_source.csv and fig4_refusal_source.csv; complete change counts are in Table 5.

**Supplementary Fig. S1 | Separate official 40-question AI evaluation.** a, AI answer-quality means on a 0–4 scale, 40 answers per condition. b, Supported statement–citation links and coverage of statements requiring citations for the two RAG conditions, based on 80 evidence ratings. Numerators and distinct denominators are in Table 2-AI40. Points show means or pooled ratios; bars show 95% paired source-group bootstrap intervals (40 groups; 2,000 resamples; seed=20261018). Intervals exclude judge error; paired differences should be read from the contrast table rather than inferred from overlap of marginal intervals. This evaluation is separate from the development 101-question audit and is not expert human evaluation. Source data: figS 1_ai40_source.csv.
