# English table legends

**Table 1 | Experimental settings and evidence scope.** Official MCQA and OpenQA share 227 original questions, with 181 primary source groups and 144 conservative groups; each task has 908 outputs across four conditions. Development comprises 101 previously selected questions in 91 released EID groups (90 after exact-passage merging), with 1,010 outputs across 10 conditions. The official 40-question AI evaluation contains 160 quality and 80 evidence ratings. The development 101-question audit covers only 202 v1/v2 RAG answers at the 160-token ceiling. Official FT is English v2 epoch_1, continued from English v1 epoch_1; historical Chinese models and v1 epoch_2 are excluded. Per-cell model, generation, retrieval, metric and raw-file details are provided in the CSV. Questions and training examples are not independent training replications.

**Table 2 | Four-condition official-test automatic evaluation.** Each condition contains 227 questions. Parsed option accuracy follows the prespecified permissive parser; strict accuracy requires a correct single-letter response; format compliance requires a single letter regardless of correctness. OpenQA F1 is mean citation-stripped lexical overlap with the original published reference, with score sum/227 supplied in the CSV. It is not factual accuracy. EM denotes citation-stripped exact match. Citation-bearing answers use 227 as denominator; ID resolution uses complete-marker counts, with 0/0 reported NA. ID resolution does not establish support. Length and truncation in the primary table refer to OpenQA, separately from MCQA at 16 tokens. Intervals are 95% paired percentile source-group bootstrap intervals (181 groups; 2,000 resamples; seed=20261017). Supplemental data retain 144-group and 225/206/21-question analyses. Fractional differences are multiplied by 100 to report pp after calculation without rounding. Raw sources are the frozen task-level answers.jsonl and original analyses; metrics and intervals were reproduced.

**Table 2-AI40 | Separately reported official-test AI evaluation.** Forty questions were preselected from the 206 with published answer_in_text=1; each condition has 40 answers. Quality and completeness are 0–4 score sums divided by 40, not binary accuracy rates. There are 160 quality and 80 RAG evidence ratings. Citation support is supported statement–citation links/all links; citation coverage is statements with a supported citation/statements requiring citations. Intervals use 40 source groups, 2,000 resamples and seed=20261018; 32-group sensitivity estimates are retained. Intervals exclude judge error. One AI process supplied the ratings; the exact judge build was not independently recorded and there were zero independent human raters. This subset is not pooled with the development audit.

**Table 3 | Development-stage comparison.** Base, v1 and v2 are evaluated with and without BM 25 RAG on the same 101 questions using a 160-token ceiling and shared evidence_v2 evaluation prompts. Continuation changes training-message alignment, sampling weight, learning rate and training amount together. F1 score sums/101 are macro-averages, not binary proportions. Answer-level rates use 101, while marker rates use marker totals. Paired question-weighted intervals use 91 groups, 10,000 resamples and seed=20261028; 90-group sensitivity results are separate. All comparisons are exploratory with unadjusted pointwise intervals.

**Table 4 | Development generation-budget sensitivity.** Base and v2 are compared with/without RAG at 160- and 96-token ceilings on the same 101 questions. No 96-token v1 condition is inferred. Generated tokens include EOS; word counts use whitespace splitting, with citation-stripped counts retained separately. Truncation means reaching the ceiling without EOS. Unclosed brackets are reported separately: unfinished identifiers are not included in complete-marker resolution denominators and are not removed by the existing citation regex. Saved prefix replay is a diagnostic, not another independent experiment. Statistics follow Table 3. Equal ceilings do not imply equal realized lengths or elimination of length effects.

**Table 5 | Single-AI development content audit and coverage sensitivity.** The audit contains 101 pairs/202 v1/v2 RAG answers at 160 tokens. Evidence sufficiency and comparative labels use 101 questions; answer-level properties use 101 answers per stage and may overlap. The AI defined 141 points over 97 questions; 4 questions remain NA. Pooled point coverage gives full/partial/omitted points weights 1/0.5/0: 104/141 and 93/141 are weighted score totals, not counts of correct facts. Equal-question sensitivity first calculates each question's proportion, then averages across 97; 75.125 and 67.875 are sums of these proportions. Point selection is subjective; unknowns are not zero. Citation support is separated into answer- and marker-level states; uncited answers are not automatically supported. S/C/N/U denote supported/contradicted/unsupported/uncertain relative to supplied passages. Original labels are preserved. No CI or significance is estimated. The judge version was unrecorded and prior result exposure precludes a fully independent blinded interpretation. Seventeen “worse” labels do not denote 17 core factual errors.

**Table 6 | Cross-tabulation of F1 direction and AI content judgments.** All 101 development RAG 160 pairs are retained. F1 difference is v2−v1, using a 1e −12 threshold; increased/decreased/unchanged row denominators are 24/35/42, and the overall denominator is 101. The five original comparative labels are preserved, including trade-off and uncertain. Unknown is not treated as equivalent or zero. Row totals and explicit denominator-specific proportions are supplied. The table describes agreement with one AI judgment, not scientific ground truth; there are no confidence intervals.

**Case table | Evidence-anchored examples of stage differences.** Eleven purposively selected questions include A1234, A1726, A718 and every newly erroneous refusal. The CSV retains full questions, actual evidence and IDs, both original answers, F1 and token lengths, original AI labels and reasons, supported interpretations and disputes. Markdown provides readable excerpts. Case frequencies do not estimate population error rates. A1109 cost scope and A1422 geological interpretation require independent specialist review.

Absolute source paths, definitions and statistical denominators are supplied in the CSVs and `metric_definitions.md`. No new gold answer, human score or judge version is inferred.
